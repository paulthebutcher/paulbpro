# Accessibility and motion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use semantic landmarks, a skip link, one h1 per page and ordered headings. Lead levels and principles remain available as h3 and h4.
- Keep all controls keyboard reachable with visible focus and readable names. Targets are at least 24×24 CSS px, with room for focus outlines. Sticky headers reserve clearance for anchors and focus.
- Body and muted text need 4.5:1 against their rendered surfaces. Check reflow at 320px, readability at 200% text zoom, and accessibility of the linked résumé.
- Work and Build keep their sticky section tags. The Lead tag and level headings scroll with their content.
- The motion vocabulary is sticky headings, rules drawing, headings rising and text settling. Scroll timelines drive Lead rules, the Work progress bar and the existing Work image reveal when supported. Fallbacks show the finished content. Keep grid and grain static while scrolling and leave technical drawings fully drawn. Ease decorative reveals with the fluid token and keep the Work progress indicator proportional to scroll. No scroll interception, snap, or parallax.
- On each homepage load and reload, the grid draws down as the headline rises through line masks. The portrait wipes up, the thesis, buttons and stats settle, then the nav slides in. Wait for both Diatype weights. Show the finished hero immediately if a font fails or the 1s wait expires. The sequence runs for about 1.8s after fonts are ready. The portrait wipe uses clipping at full opacity to keep the image eligible for LCP. Keep focused links and their labels visible throughout. Reduced motion and no JS show the finished state. Check mobile and desktop LCP against the pre-change page.
- Hero stat values stay in the foreground color. On hover or keyboard focus, the caption changes to the accent and a small arrow in the same color eases in beside its final word. Touch shows this state by default. Reduced motion shows the arrow immediately.
- An IntersectionObserver starts each text reveal once. Authored heading lines rise through masks when they match the layout. On narrow layouts or when a line wraps, the whole heading rises through a single mask. Intros, principles and card text settle as blocks. Mono labels wipe left to right.
- The Lead closing statement is the only letter reveal. Words stay together and a visually hidden copy provides the heading’s accessible name. The note settles 175 ms after the last letter. Focus finishes any reveal on the focused element or card.
