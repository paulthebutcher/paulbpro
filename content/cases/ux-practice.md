## Situation

A $750M portfolio of enterprise cyber products with no UX function, seven net-new product teams moving through the company’s Studio lifecycle, and design showing up late or not at all.

## My role

Hired and placed one lead per revenue product, three direct and five matrixed. Built the input framework teams used before each Studio gate. Took on customer analytics and the desirability review because nobody owned them.

We stood up design, research and customer analytics from zero. I handled all intake myself, working with each requesting team as a strategist so designers got clear briefs and partners got timelines they could plan around. My team didn’t miss a deadline on its own deliverables in my time there.

I kept a capability map of everyone’s skills and gaps. Once a quarter, outside regular 1:1s, each person and I co-wrote their goals against what the business needed. Everyone owned a product and was the team’s go-to for one specialty.

Beyond the team, on the global CX&D Guild board I co-wrote recommendations on how design teams are structured, with the global head of design, and built the deck with input from him and two of his VPs. It went to several EVPs and the Chief Innovation Officer.

## What changed

Time to first gate roughly halved for the seven new teams. Nine functions used the dashboards. Design had a seat at the venture review instead of a slot after it.
