## Why it exists

A public-adjusting firm starts every claim with a pile of documents: photos, forms and a policy. The app turns that pile into one structured claim file.

## What I built

It was distilled and architected in Foundry from the firm’s executive summary. The extraction runs locally and gives the same answer every time. It replaced an earlier pattern-matching version that broke on real documents. It passed all 22 tests and all 8 acceptance criteria.

## Where it is now

A working demo that doesn’t capture real data. During the build, Foundry’s legal agent flagged that the business model needs a lawyer’s sign-off before it can serve customers, and the firm is still working that out.

## What’s still hard

Whether the service can run at all is a legal question, and it’s still open.
