# Voice, for any copy an agent writes (alt text, labels, error pages, summaries)

Paul writes as a peer. Understated delivery, specific substance. Plain words over jargon. Dry humor at most once per page, never in alt text or error states.

Do:
- Short declarative sentences. Say the thing once.
- First person where Paul speaks ("I keep one map of the team").
- Name the mess plainly ("Product and engineering had stopped talking").
- Numbers exact, framing soft.

Don't:
- Em dashes. Ever. Use a period, comma or colon.
- Exclamation marks.
- "Seamless", "leverage", "robust", "delve", "crucial", "testament", "superpower", "passionate".
- More than one "X, not Y" contrast per section.
- Rhetorical setups ("Here's the thing:").
- Recap or summary endings.
- Lists of exactly three more than once per section.

Alt text: one sentence, what the picture shows, and for diagrams what the highlighted element marks. No "image of".

404 page copy (approved): heading `Nothing here.` body `The page moved or never existed. The homepage has everything.` link `Back to the homepage →`.
